# Explorer RDKit fallback: Step 3

The frozen offline v1 benchmark was replayed with `PYTHONPATH=. .venv/bin/python tests/explorer_3d_step3_v1.py`. Results remain **8/16** displayable: 7 converged, structurally checked RDKit candidates and the existing idealized ZrCl4 gas reference. The original offline baseline was also 8/16. This is **zero measured coverage gain** on these cases; the benefit demonstrated by a controlled test is recovery when the first embedding attempt fails. PubChem availability was not tested by this offline benchmark. A passed graph/stereo/coordinate check does not establish experimental geometry.

SF6 has no supported UFF parameters in this environment; Rh and Zr remain outside the covalent-element allowlist, with Zr retaining the separate existing exact-CID reference. The selected iron pentacarbonyl representation fails RDKit input parsing. Charged species and radicals remain excluded; opposite-charge disconnected assemblies remain information-only. No claim is made about a different representation or physical form of these substances.

Explorer's local fallback now uses at most three deterministic ETKDGv3 embedding attempts, with two starting seeds and a final random-coordinate start, followed by at most 600 force-field optimization iterations per attempt. MMFF94 is preferred when all parameters exist; UFF is selected only otherwise when all its parameters exist. A candidate is returned only when optimization converges and checks pass for molecular graph, atom index/identity, specified stereochemistry reconstructed from 3D, finite coordinates, severe overlaps, and bounded bonded distances. These bounds are coarse sanity checks, not accurate reference-geometry tests. Failed candidates remain unavailable and no invented approximate 3D result is presented. Unspecified stereo remains an unresolved model limitation.

The Builder and Reaction Lab use their existing geometry path and are not changed. PubChem models and the existing zirconium reference keep their previous priority and provenance labels.
